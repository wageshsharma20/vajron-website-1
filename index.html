Autonomous mission sequence

  1. Pre-flight verificationHealth, calibration, battery and environment checks before arming.
  2. Safe take-offOverhead clearance confirmed by the 40 m scan; gear retracts after lift-off.
  3. Obstacle-aware flightTerrain-aware path planning with fused vision and LiDAR, and dynamic safety buffers around moving people or vehicles.
  4. Autonomous returnSmart return-to-launch triggers automatically if battery or conditions change mid-mission.
